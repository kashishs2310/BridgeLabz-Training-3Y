Words That Inspire

On Perseverance

Success is not final, failure is not fatal: it is the courage to continue that counts.

— Winston Churchill

On Learning

Live as if you were to die tomorrow. Learn as if you were to live forever.

— Mahatma Gandhi

On Innovation

Innovation distinguishes between a leader and a follower.

— Steve Jobs

Quotes inspire people because they distill hard-earned wisdom into a few memorable words, giving us perspective and motivation when we need it most.
