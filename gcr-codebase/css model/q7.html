The Art of Readable Type

A short exploration of type, weight, and rhythm on the web.

Good typography is invisible when it works, and distracting when it doesn't. Spacing, size, and rhythm all quietly shape how a reader moves through a page.

Line height controls the breathing room between lines, while letter spacing adjusts how tightly characters sit next to each other. Used well, both make long passages easier to follow.
